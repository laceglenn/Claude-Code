# Tenth Workday Schedule

Records the calculated 10th business day of each month, accounting for
weekends and Swiss federal / Zurich cantonal public holidays.

| Month | Year | 10th Business Day |
|-------|------|--------------------|
| September | 2026 | 2026-09-14 (Monday) |

## Notes

- **September 2026**: No Swiss federal or Zurich cantonal public holidays
  fall within the counting window. Knabenschiessen (Sept 14, 2026) is a
  local Zurich city tradition (a half-day observance for schools and
  municipal offices) and is not a statutory canton-wide public holiday, so
  it was not excluded from the business-day count.
  Business days counted: Sep 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 14.
